Ideas

Money that moves between players 7

1

Danfo fleet owner

Buy a danfo, hire real players to drive it, and collect a daily remit while they decide whether to remit complete.

Why it fits it is the most Lagos side hustle there is, and it puts salary, trust and cheating between two real people.

ideaMedium build
2

Ajo and esusu circles

Six players pay in every day, one takes the whole pot each round, and the order is fixed by the group head. Defaulters get named in the contacts app.

Why it fits this is how people actually save here, and it only works if players keep their word.

ideaMedium build
3

Aso-ebi tailor

Players with the tailor skill take fabric orders from other players and sew the outfit, then the buyer pays and wears it at the next owambe.

Why it fits gives a real reason for the wardrobe system to have a maker and a buyer.

ideaMedium build
4

Market stalls and bale trading

Rent a stall at a market, buy a bale of okrika at the wholesale price, sort it, and sell piece by piece to other players and NPCs. Prices move with how many stalls sell the same thing.

Why it fits Balogun and Yaba market are the heart of the hustle.

ideaLarge build
5

Mile 12 to Oshodi supply run

Real players haul tomatoes, peppers and yam from the farm market to the sellers, and the delivery price rises when the road is flooded or the checkpoint is hot.

Why it fits logistics with other players on the road is the best use of the open map.

ideaLarge build
6

Prepaid token reseller

Buy units in bulk from the Lagos Power app and resell to neighbours who just ran out at night, with a small markup.

Why it fits it hooks into the grid we already have and turns every blackout into a business.

ideaSmall build
7

Sachet water factory

Own the machine, buy the roll and the water, and supply every hawker on the map. A bad batch gets people sick and the area remembers your brand name.

Why it fits pure water is a Lagos staple, and it is a clean small business to grow into a big one.

ideaMedium build

Things you can own and run 7

8

Your own pepper soup joint

Set the menu and the prices, hire real players as cook and waiter, and put a screen on for the match.

Why it fits Lagos nights happen in joints, and we already have the room.

ideaMedium build
9

Owambe host

Book a hall, send invites through the contacts app, set the dress code, hire a DJ and a MC, and let guests spray naira. The host keeps a cut of the spray.

Why it fits a party you throw yourself is the best show off there is.

ideaMedium build
10

Ministry and branch church or mosque

Found a small branch, set the service times, preach or lead prayers, and collect offerings from real players who attend.

Why it fits faith is part of daily life here, so it should be a place players gather, not just a building.

ideaLarge build
11

Radio station

Own a frequency, hire presenters, play licensed music, and sell ad slots through MyAds. Every car on the road can tune in.

Why it fits it makes the soundscape a player economy.

ideaMedium build
12

Car showroom and plate auction

Park your best cars in a glass showroom that others can walk through, and auction clean plate numbers to the highest bidder.

Why it fits flexing a car is a sport, and a plate is a status symbol.

ideaMedium build
13

Estate security crew

Residents of a compound pay a monthly levy to a crew of players who patrol at night. Do the job well and the estate renews, do it badly and the thieves know the gate is open.

Why it fits it is a real Lagos business and it is a gentle way into the police career.

ideaMedium build
14

Ferry and boat owner

Own a boat on the Ikorodu to Mile 12 or Victoria Island lines, set the fare, and carry real players over the lagoon when the road is jammed.

Why it fits the water is half of Lagos and is mostly empty on our map.

ideaMedium build

Detty December calendar

16

Lagos Gist feed

Players post screenshots and short clips of their day, others vote, and the most talked about posts earn fame that unlocks club entry and brand deals.

Why it fits show off is the point, and fame becomes a currency.

ideaMedium build
17

Night drag strip and car meet

A weekly meet at a long road in Lekki where players race, spectators place bets with each other, and the police may or may not show up.

Why it fits car culture, betting and chaos in one night.

ideaMedium build
18

Best amala in Lagos rankings

Players rate every food spot they eat at and the city publishes a weekly top ten. Owners can pay to run a promotion, and a bad score costs real customers.

Why it fits Lagos argues about food all day, so let it argue in game.

ideaSmall build
19

Wedding planner career

Players plan a couple's wedding from the venue to the cake to the guest list, and the couple, both real players, rate the day.

Why it fits it builds on the owambe system and gives a social career that pays well.

Pressure from the city 7

20

LASTMA raid days

Some days the town is hot with LASTMA, okada riders reroute, and towing trucks are out. Players on the job get a cut, players on the road feel the fear.

Why it fits it shows both sides of one law.

ideaSmall build
21

Fuel scarcity weeks

Petrol stations run dry, queues stretch for blocks, and the black market sells jerrycans at three times the price.

Why it fits it forces hard choices about driving, generators and who you trust.

ideaSmall build
22

Flood season

Heavy rain floods low areas such as Lekki and Ikoyi roads, cars stall, wading players earn from pushing, and boats appear. Insurance exists and is a headache.

Why it fits Lagos in July is a real thing and we already have rain.

ideaMedium build
23

Neighbourhood reputation

Each area keeps a score for you based on what you did there, and good standing opens doors such as a discount, a loan or a quiet word before trouble.

Why it fits deep Lagos life means the street remembers you.

ideaMedium build
24

Ride hail with real riders

Players call each other for rides, set a fare, rate each other, and surge pricing kicks in when it rains.

Why it fits it is the most natural way for two strangers to meet and trade.

ideaMedium build
25

Landlord and tenant chaos

Rent due dates, caution fees, a leak nobody wants to fix, and an agent who disappears with the deposit. Players can be any side of it.

Why it fits every Lagos person has a landlord story.

ideaMedium build

Generator fumes and noise complaints

A loud generator in a tight compound angers neighbours, who can call the landlord or settle it with a bottle of something.

Why it fits a small system that makes the blackout feel social.
