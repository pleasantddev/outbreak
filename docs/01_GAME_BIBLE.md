# NIGHTFALL: Game Bible

Working title. Season 1: The Awakening.

> Lagos was already dangerous. Then something woke up.

## 1. The pitch in thirty seconds

Lagos has fallen, but it has not died. Twelve survivors drop into a supernatural version of Oshodi at dusk. They scavenge guns, vehicles and strange abilities while fighting each other and the things that used to be their neighbours. As night deepens, the city collapses around them. Then the Heart wakes up somewhere under the streets, and every survivor hears it.

Whoever takes the Heart becomes the most wanted person in the city. Their position pulses onto every map. Creatures turn toward them. Everyone else has a choice: hunt the carrier, steal the Heart, run, or use the chaos to finish a private job.

Get the Heart. Reach extraction. Survive the hunt. Or become the monster everyone fears.

## 2. Thesis

**Player fantasy.** You are a Lagos survivor who knows the city better than the thing that broke it. You can hijack a danfo, take a rooftop, fold into shadow, or become a monster for forty five seconds.

**Core promise.** Every match ends in a fight over one object, so the last minutes always converge on a place, a person and a decision.

**Differentiator.** The win condition moves. Last player standing still wins, but carrying the Heart out wins faster, and a secret contract can let you walk out alone. Players stop behaving like interchangeable shooters because they want different things.

**Pillars.**

| Pillar | What it changes | What it cuts |
|---|---|---|
| The city is a weapon | Enterable multi storey buildings, part based vehicle damage, generators, fuel, corruption | Flat arenas, decorative props |
| Everybody knows | Heart and Awakened pings, gunfire on the minimap, server announcements | Silent kills with no consequence |
| Power has a price | Awakening, the Heart and the Runner rig all trade safety for strength | Pure upgrades, pay to win |
| Lagos, not "African city" | Danfos, okadas, buka signboards, GeePee water tanks, under bridge markets, Ankara and Adire prints | Generic post apocalypse with a texture swap |
| Bloody darkness | Night lighting, red fog, Stalkers that hate light, gore and grime | Bright readable daylight as the default |

**Core loop.** DROP, LOOT, FIGHT, ADAPT to the changing city, CONVERGE on the Heart, EXTRACT or HUNT, REPEAT.

**Session loop.** A twenty minute match: drop, survive, corruption, the Heart, extraction, collapse.

**Long term loop.** Match results feed XP and Naira, which unlock cosmetic finishes, operators and seasonal story. Nothing permanent makes a player stronger inside a match.

**Emotional curve.** Tense quiet on landing, opportunistic fights while looting, unease as districts go red and lights die, a hard spike when the Heart wakes, then a chaotic last stand at an extraction point.

**The design question the prototype answers.** Is one twenty minute match so tense and chaotic in its final five minutes that people want to queue again straight away?

## 3. How a match plays

| Phase | Default time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Drop | 0:00 | Pick a landing spot on the district map. Land with your archetype kit, a sidearm, a cutlass, two bandages and your archetype ability. |
| Survival | 1:00 | Loot buildings, find a primary, hear distant gunfire. Hollow stand motionless at bus stops. |
| Corruption | 5:00 | Two districts turn red. Corrupted ground hurts and spits out creatures. One district suffers a blackout and its lamps die. |
| The Heart | 10:00 | The screen goes quiet, the ground shakes, a pulse crosses the city and the sky stains red. A pillar of red light marks the Heart. |
| Extraction | 12:00 | Airport Road helipad, Apapa Port quay and the Lagoon jetty open. |
| Collapse | 16:00 | A wall of red fog closes the city toward a final circle. |
| End | 20:00 | The circle keeps shrinking until somebody wins. |

Match length is a setting from six to twenty minutes. Every phase scales with it.

## 4. Ways to win

**A. Extract the Heart.** Pick it up, carry it to an open extraction and hold the zone for thirty seconds. Leave the zone and the timer bleeds away. The carrier gets fifty extra health and regenerates, moves eight percent slower, pings onto every map every ten seconds and pulls creatures within sixty metres. If the carrier dies, the Heart drops where they fell.

**B. Last survivor.** If nobody gets the Heart out, the last person breathing wins.

**C. Contract victory.** Every player receives a secret contract. Complete it and you may slip out through any open extraction by holding it for twenty seconds. That ends your match as a win without ending everybody else's.

| Contract | Brief |
|---|---|
| The Hunter | Kill three survivors. |
| The Butcher | Put down twelve creatures. |
| The Smuggler | Carry ₦500,000 of contraband to an open extraction. |
| The Thief | Hold the Heart for twenty seconds, however you get it. |
| The Survivor | Be alive when the Heart wakes and never Awaken. |
| The Informant | Stay within thirty five metres of a named player for sixty seconds without killing them. |

## 5. Awakening: becoming the monster

Two Awakening Artifacts spawn per match, always on high floors or rooftops. Use one and you transform for forty five seconds:

* 150 extra health and fast regeneration
* 55 percent faster movement and nearly double jump height
* Claws that deal 70 damage in a cone
* Wall climbing: push into any wall and you go up it
* Monster vision that shows every living survivor within seventy metres through walls

The whole server is told: **AN AWAKENED HAS ENTERED THE CITY**. Your position pings every eight seconds. Kill an Awakened and you get its Core, which refills and ranks up your abilities, gives you full Level 3 armour and restores your health.

## 6. Archetypes and loadouts

Archetypes are starting kits, not classes. Everyone also lands with the Ogun 9 sidearm, a cutlass and two bandages, and anyone can change build during a match. Signature weapons come with one magazine and a small reserve, so looting still matters.

| Archetype | Lands with | Ability | Perk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scout | Okada SMG | Shadow | Sprints 8 percent faster, quieter footsteps |
| Hunter | Marina AR | Force | Fresh enemy footprints glow, pings last longer |
| Enforcer | Third Mainland 12 | Metal | Lands with a light vest |
| Runner | Ogun 9 | Force | **Courier rig: 10 backpack slots instead of 6**, a contraband pouch that stacks three bundles per slot, mounts vehicles in half the time |

The Runner is the class with extra inventory space. Couriers, smugglers and the people who move things through Lagos carry more, which makes them the natural Smuggler and the best squad supplier once squads arrive.

## 7. Inventory

Fixed slots keep looting fast: Primary, Secondary, Melee, Ability 1, Ability 2, Throwable, Armour and Backpack. Ammunition, healing, contraband, frags, repair kits, jerrycans and artifacts all compete for backpack slots, so the Runner's four extra slots are a real decision rather than a number.

## 8. Weapons and attachments

| Weapon | Role |
|---|---|
| Ogun 9 | Reliable sidearm, everyone lands with one |
| Okada SMG | Close quarters, very fast |
| Marina AR | General purpose, the best all rounder |
| Third Mainland 12 | Room clearer, brutal indoors |
| Lekki Longshot | Rooftop sniper, one shot to the head through most armour |
| Cutlass | Silent melee |

Every attachment trades something: a suppressor is quieter but loses range, an extended magazine reloads slower, a laser tightens hip fire but shows your position, a compensator calms recoil and doubles as a dinner bell. Your armory finishes and attachment choices follow you onto any gun of that type you pick up in the city.

## 9. Supernatural abilities

Abilities change how you use guns rather than replacing them.

| Ability | Effect | Pairs with |
|---|---|---|
| Force | A ten metre cone of pressure. Throws people, flips okadas, shoves danfos, shatters Metal walls, stuns creatures. | Shotgun: throw them, then close the gap |
| Shadow | Four seconds folded into the dark, plus a six metre dash. Firing breaks it. | SMG: vanish, flank, delete |
| Metal | Rips roofing and rebar up into a three metre bullet stopping wall for sixteen seconds. | LMG or AR: instant cover on open roads |

Ability Shards found in the city add a second ability or rank an existing one up to three times.

## 10. Vehicles

Vehicles are built from parts: engine, four tyres, fuel and body.

* Shoot a tyre flat and the vehicle loses grip and top speed.
* Hit the engine and it smokes, then stalls, then burns, then explodes five seconds later.
* Hit the fuel tank and it leaks.
* Anyone can hijack a slow vehicle by dragging its driver out.
* Passengers can shoot. Drivers can run people and creatures over.

| Vehicle | Character |
|---|---|
| Okada | Two people, fast, fragile, leans into corners |
| Danfo | Six seats, slow, loud horn, takes a beating, carries the squad and the whole server's attention |
| Wahala SUV | Four seats, quick and tough, the big man jeep |

## 11. Creatures

| Creature | Behaviour | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Crawler | Hunched and fast, hunts in packs, low health | Shotguns, Force |
| Hollow | Neighbours who stopped. They stand motionless until you move near, then come for you slowly and hit hard. Stronger at night. | Headshots, distance |
| Stalker | Only appears in deep night. Tall, thin, black skin cracked with red light. Very fast and very dangerous. It freezes while a flashlight beam holds it. | Keep your light on it and back away |

Creatures hear gunfire, explosions and sprinting. They swarm toward the Heart carrier and pour out of corrupted districts.

## 12. The city

The prototype map is a fictional Oshodi district, 720 by 720 metres. It is sized so a sprinting survivor crosses it in about a minute and a half and an okada does it in under thirty seconds.

* **Oshodi Terminal.** A huge concrete canopy over bus bays, wrecked danfos, a two storey waiting hall.
* **Ile Epo Market.** Dense stalls with corrugated roofs and alleys barely wide enough to walk through. Close fights and fire barrels.
* **The Flyover.** An elevated expressway seven and a half metres up, with ramps at both ends and an under bridge market beneath it.
* **Mainland Estates.** Two to four storey blocks, mechanic yards, the drainage canal.
* **Central Oshodi.** Taller buildings, up to five storeys, better rooftop loot.
* **Apapa Port.** Container stacks, gantry cranes, a warehouse and the port extraction.
* **Lagoon Edge.** Stilt shacks, canoes, plank walkways and the jetty extraction over dark water you can swim in.
* **Airport Road.** Hangars, a fence line and the helipad extraction.

Every building can be entered. Each has a switchback stairwell from the ground floor to the roof, interior rooms, furniture, loot on every floor, and a rooftop with parapets, GeePee water tanks and satellite dishes.

## 13. Night and horror

Matches start at dusk under a blood moon and sink into deep night by the time the Heart wakes. Sodium lamps flicker and die. Blackouts kill whole districts. Generators hum in the dark, and the light around them is the only safe light. The horror lives in stillness: a Hollow at the end of a street that is gone when you look back, a scream three streets away, footsteps upstairs in a building you thought was empty.

## 14. Factions (later)

Factions influence the world instead of acting as classes.

| Faction | Identity |
|---|---|
| The Wayfarers | Neutral survivor network: information, trade, extraction |
| The Runners | Couriers and smugglers, vehicles and contraband contracts |
| The Wardens | Armed survivors who sell protection and post bounties |
| The Foundry | Engineers: weapons, vehicles, technology |
| The Hollow | People who embraced the supernatural, and pray to the Awakened |

## 15. Progression and economy

Permanent progression is cosmetic and expressive: operators, outfits, prints, weapon finishes, charms, titles and lore. Some finishes unlock by level (Nightfall at 3, Bloodwork at 5, Bone Charm at 7, Big Man Gold at 10). XP and Naira come from kills, creature kills, placement, survival time and contraband carried out. Matches always start fair.

## 16. Lore

Something has always been under Lagos. Three nights before the event the deep water of the lagoon turned warm and the fishermen of Makoko pulled up fish with no eyes. At 2:14 one morning every phone in the city lost signal and a sound came up through the ground, low enough to feel in your teeth.

People began to change. Some stopped moving and became the Hollow. A few touched the shards and lived, and became Awakened. The rest of the world began seeing the same phenomena, but Lagos became one of the worst places on Earth.

Nobody knows what the Heart is or why it sits beneath Lagos. Everybody knows what it does: whoever carries it out can sell it for anything they want. Nobody who has sold it has been seen again.

## 18. What the prototype deliberately leaves out

Huge persistent progression, hundreds of weapons, crafting, housing, guilds, quest chains, a hundred player server, several cities and a story campaign. The long term world, factions and settlements remain the plan once the core match proves itself.
