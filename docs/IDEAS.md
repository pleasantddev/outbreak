# HustleLife ideas

A running list of ideas in the founder's spirit: deep Lagos life, online play, money that moves between real players, things you can own, and things you run and show off. Each one has a one line pitch, why it fits, a rough size and a status.

Size: S is a few days, M is a week or two, L is a big build. Status: idea, building, shipped.

Rules every idea has to respect: 16+, no nudity, no real brand logos, parody names for real places and clubs, crime is satire and never a how-to, music only where we have the right to play it.

## Money that moves between players

1. **Danfo fleet owner.** Buy a danfo, hire real players to drive it, and collect a daily remit while they decide whether to remit complete. Why: it is the most Lagos side hustle there is, and it puts salary, trust and cheating between two real people. Size M. Status: idea.

2. **Ajo and esusu circles.** Six players pay in every day, one takes the whole pot each round, and the order is fixed by the group head. Defaulters get named in the contacts app. Why: this is how people actually save here, and it only works if players keep their word. Size M. Status: idea.

3. **Aso-ebi tailor.** Players with the tailor skill take fabric orders from other players and sew the outfit, then the buyer pays and wears it at the next owambe. Why: gives a real reason for the wardrobe system to have a maker and a buyer. Size M. Status: idea.

4. **Market stalls and bale trading.** Rent a stall at a market, buy a bale of okrika at the wholesale price, sort it, and sell piece by piece to other players and NPCs. Prices move with how many stalls sell the same thing. Why: Balogun and Yaba market are the heart of the hustle. Size L. Status: idea.

5. **Mile 12 to Oshodi supply run.** Real players haul tomatoes, peppers and yam from the farm market to the sellers, and the delivery price rises when the road is flooded or the checkpoint is hot. Why: logistics with other players on the road is the best use of the open map. Size L. Status: idea.

6. **Prepaid token reseller.** Buy units in bulk from the Lagos Power app and resell to neighbours who just ran out at night, with a small markup. Why: it hooks into the grid we already have and turns every blackout into a business. Size S. Status: idea.

7. **Sachet water factory.** Own the machine, buy the roll and the water, and supply every hawker on the map. A bad batch gets people sick and the area remembers your brand name. Why: pure water is a Lagos staple, and it is a clean small business to grow into a big one. Size M. Status: idea.

## Things you can own and run

8. **Your own pepper soup joint.** Set the menu and the prices, hire real players as cook and waiter, and put a screen on for the match. Why: Lagos nights happen in joints, and we already have the room. Size M. Status: idea.

9. **Owambe host.** Book a hall, send invites through the contacts app, set the dress code, hire a DJ and a MC, and let guests spray naira. The host keeps a cut of the spray. Why: a party you throw yourself is the best show off there is. Size M. Status: idea.

10. **Ministry and branch church or mosque.** Found a small branch, set the service times, preach or lead prayers, and collect offerings from real players who attend. Why: faith is part of daily life here, so it should be a place players gather, not just a building. Size L. Status: idea.

11. **Radio station.** Own a frequency, hire presenters, play licensed music, and sell ad slots through MyAds. Every car on the road can tune in. Why: it makes the soundscape a player economy. Size M. Status: idea.

12. **Car showroom and plate auction.** Park your best cars in a glass showroom that others can walk through, and auction clean plate numbers to the highest bidder. Why: flexing a car is a sport, and a plate is a status symbol. Size M. Status: idea.

13. **Estate security crew.** Residents of a compound pay a monthly levy to a crew of players who patrol at night. Do the job well and the estate renews, do it badly and the thieves know the gate is open. Why: it is a real Lagos business and it is a gentle way into the police career. Size M. Status: idea.

14. **Ferry and boat owner.** Own a boat on the Ikorodu to Mile 12 or Victoria Island lines, set the fare, and carry real players over the lagoon when the road is jammed. Why: the water is half of Lagos and is mostly empty on our map. Size M. Status: idea.

## Run it and show it off

15. **Detty December calendar.** A server wide event calendar with concerts, beach parties and carnival closures that change traffic, prices and what is open. Why: one shared Lagos with one shared calendar makes the world feel alive. Size L. Status: idea.

16. **Lagos Gist feed.** Players post screenshots and short clips of their day, others vote, and the most talked about posts earn fame that unlocks club entry and brand deals. Why: show off is the point, and fame becomes a currency. Size M. Status: idea.

17. **Night drag strip and car meet.** A weekly meet at a long road in Lekki where players race, spectators place bets with each other, and the police may or may not show up. Why: car culture, betting and chaos in one night. Size M. Status: idea.

18. **Best amala in Lagos rankings.** Players rate every food spot they eat at and the city publishes a weekly top ten. Owners can pay to run a promotion, and a bad score costs real customers. Why: Lagos argues about food all day, so let it argue in game. Size S. Status: idea.

19. **Wedding planner career.** Players plan a couple's wedding from the venue to the cake to the guest list, and the couple, both real players, rate the day. Why: it builds on the owambe system and gives a social career that pays well. Size M. Status: idea.

## Pressure from the city

20. **LASTMA raid days.** Some days the town is hot with LASTMA, okada riders reroute, and towing trucks are out. Players on the job get a cut, players on the road feel the fear. Why: it shows both sides of one law. Size S. Status: idea.

21. **Fuel scarcity weeks.** Petrol stations run dry, queues stretch for blocks, and the black market sells jerrycans at three times the price. Why: it forces hard choices about driving, generators and who you trust. Size S. Status: idea.

22. **Flood season.** Heavy rain floods low areas such as Lekki and Ikoyi roads, cars stall, wading players earn from pushing, and boats appear. Insurance exists and is a headache. Why: Lagos in July is a real thing and we already have rain. Size M. Status: idea.

23. **Neighbourhood reputation.** Each area keeps a score for you based on what you did there, and good standing opens doors such as a discount, a loan or a quiet word before trouble. Why: deep Lagos life means the street remembers you. Size M. Status: idea.

24. **Ride hail with real riders.** Players call each other for rides, set a fare, rate each other, and surge pricing kicks in when it rains. Why: it is the most natural way for two strangers to meet and trade. Size M. Status: idea.

25. **Landlord and tenant chaos.** Rent due dates, caution fees, a leak nobody wants to fix, and an agent who disappears with the deposit. Players can be any side of it. Why: every Lagos person has a landlord story. Size M. Status: idea.

26. **Generator fumes and noise complaints.** A loud generator in a tight compound angers neighbours, who can call the landlord or settle it with a bottle of something. Why: a small system that makes the blackout feel social. Size S. Status: idea.

## Sickness, luck and the body (from the founder's brief 4)

27. **Silent carrier.** Luck decides who carries a spreading illness without feeling it, so a player can pass a cough on at the market and never know it was them. Why: the founder wants sickness that travels between real players, and the quiet carrier is the fun, scary version of it. Size L. Status: idea.

28. **Clinic and pharmacy you own.** Open a clinic or a chemist, hire real players as nurse and pharmacist, set your own fees, and keep the drugs stocked from Mile 12 side suppliers. Why: hospitals are a money sink for players, so owning one is the other side of the same coin. Size M. Status: idea.

29. **Ambulance runs.** A player with a van and a siren races a collapsed player to the nearest hospital and takes a cut of the bill for getting there alive. Why: it turns the hospital respawn into a job another player can do for you. Size M. Status: idea.

30. **Babalawo and prayer mountain.** Pay for a reading or a night of prayer and get a vague hint about your luck, which may or may not be true. Why: the luck meter stays hidden, and Lagos has a whole trade in guessing at it, played here as satire. Size M. Status: idea.

31. **Optician and fashion frames.** Glasses cure the edge blur of myopia, and players who sell frames make a name for the style. Why: it gives the negative traits a fix that is also a shop worth owning. Size S. Status: idea.

32. **Debt follows you out of the hospital.** If you cannot pay the bill you leave in underwear with a debt, and a recovery agent can chase it for a fee. Why: it makes the stripped discharge a story with a second act, never a dead end. Size M. Status: idea.

33. **Fake drug scare.** Now and then a batch of fake malaria tablets hits the pharmacies, and a regulator character shuts shops down until the owners prove they are clean. Why: it makes pharmacy owners care about their suppliers, in the same spirit as the bad sachet water batch. Size S. Status: idea.

## Rooms, phones and what breaks

34. **Face-me-I-face-you.** A shared compound with a landlord, one bathroom, a queue for the tap and a levy for the generator, where your neighbours are real players. Why: renting is where every Lagos person starts, and sharing a corridor is the best place for trouble and friendship. Size M. Status: idea.

35. **Sublet and roommates.** Split a flat with other players, divide the rent, and find out who left the light on. Why: it spreads the rent sink across friends and gives the contacts app something to do. Size M. Status: idea.

36. **Computer Village repair stalls.** Players run screen repair and battery swap stalls in Ikeja, buy parts from other players, and compete on price and speed. Why: cracked phones need a place to go, and Computer Village is the place. Size M. Status: idea.

37. **Tokunbo phones.** Used phones are graded by battery, cracks and history, and a bad grade can hide a bad history. Why: second hand is how most people buy a phone here, and it gives the repair stalls stock. Size S. Status: idea.

38. **The cloned phone cat and mouse.** A hacker character clones a phone as a game mechanic and the owner can spot the signs, clean the phone at a shop or switch phones. Why: it is satire about phone fraud with a clear fix for the victim, and never a how-to. Size M. Status: idea.

## Already in the game or on its way

39. **The power grid and generators.** Feeders go down, windows and street lamps go dark, and homes with a generator glow warm. Why: NEPA is the daily joke, so the whole map feels it. Size L. Status: shipped.

40. **Motor parks with unions and touts.** Corrugated canopies, painted kerbs, a union shed with a hand-painted sign, passengers waiting and touts working the queue. Why: the motor park is where Lagos moves. Size M. Status: shipped.

41. **Billboards you can book.** Every sellable face on the map has a stable id, size and tier, and MyAds sells them for real money. Why: players want to show off, and a brand on a bridge is the loudest way to do it. Size L. Status: shipped.

42. **Oshodi Transport Interchange.** Three six-level terminals with open bays, a ticketing hall, four parking floors, a union chairman's office on the ground floor and covered footbridges over the expressway. Why: it is the home district and the founder asked for it by name. Size L. Status: building.

43. **ZILOX, GELE and ORUN.** Three parody clubs with a DJ booth and pole stages each: a big Ikoyi superclub, a lounge, and a rooftop deck on a 21 level tower. Why: players can DJ and dance for pay, and the clubs need real places to stand. Size L. Status: building.
