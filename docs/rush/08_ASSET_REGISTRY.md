# Lagos Rush: Asset Registry

Most of Lagos Rush is generated by our own code on the player's device: textures, buildings, billboards, sounds and music. The car bodies are the exception. Fourteen of them are models from Sketchfab under CC BY 4.0, renamed, stripped of badges and plates, simplified and recoloured by `tools/cars/process.mjs`. Everything from outside is listed here, checked on 8 October 2026.

Status: GREEN means the licence is explicit and our use fits it. YELLOW means usable with an open question. RED never ships.

## Why the imported car bodies are YELLOW

The licences are clear: each creator chose CC BY 4.0 on Sketchfab, which allows commercial use and changes as long as the creator is named. The open question is the car designs themselves. Each model copies the shape of a real car whose maker holds design and trademark rights. We remove badges, maker lettering and foreign plates, rename every car under an invented Nigerian maker, and change the paint, but the silhouettes stay recognisable. That is a business risk the owner has accepted for now. Before a commercial launch, either license the designs, replace the models with original ones, or alter the bodies further. Some uploads on Sketchfab are also ripped from other games; none of these show signs of that, but the uploader's licence is only as good as their right to grant it.
